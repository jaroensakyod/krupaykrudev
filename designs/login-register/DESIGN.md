# KruPayKru Design System

## Brand
Thai education marketplace "ครูเปย์ครู KruPayKru" — teachers buying teaching materials from teachers.
Tone: trustworthy, warm, professional-but-friendly. Like Etsy x Shopee but refined for educators.

## Colors
- Primary Deep Teal: #0F766E (buttons, links, active states)
- Accent Marigold: #F59E0B (badges, highlights, sale tags)
- Success Green: #16A34A · Danger Red: #DC2626
- Background Warm White: #FAFAF8 · Cards: #FFFFFF
- Text Charcoal: #1F2937 · Muted Gray: #6B7280
- Seller badge gold: #D97706 "ครูตัวจริง" · verified blue: #2563EB "ยืนยันตัวตน"
- KruPass brand color: gradient teal→emerald for subscription elements

## Typography
All UI copy in Thai language. Headings: Kanit (fallback IBM Plex Sans Thai). Body: Sarabun (fallback Noto Sans Thai). Prices always shown with ฿ prefix.

## Shape & Components
- Cards 12px radius, soft shadow, white surface
- Pill-shaped buttons and filter chips (999px)
- Product cards: cover image 4:3, title 2-line clamp, store name + badge, star rating, price bold
- Badges: 🏅ครูตัวจริง (gold outline), 🟦ยืนยันตัวตน (blue), 🏛สถาบัน (gray), ฟรี (orange solid), มีส่วนผสม AI (gray outline), KruPass (teal gradient)

## Layout Rules
- Buyer pages: top navbar (logo, big search center, wishlist/cart/bell/avatar right)
- Seller/Admin pages: left sidebar navigation dark teal #0B4F4A
- Grids: 4-col desktop product grid, 2-col mobile
- Mobile: bottom tab bar 5 items (หน้าแรก/ค้นหา/ตะกร้า/Kluangสื่อ/โปรไฟล์)
